Caste, income and residence certificates in Bihar under RTPS: time limits and appeals (checked October 2026)

Last checked: 28 October 2026

For anyone in Bihar who needs a caste, income or residence certificate for an admission, a job, a scholarship or a scheme.

In Bihar you apply for a caste, income or residence certificate on the RTPS portal serviceonline.bihar.gov.in or at an RTPS counter. Under the Bihar Right to Public Services Act, 2011 the Circle Officer must issue it within 10 working days, or 2 working days under Tatkal. If it is late, appeal to the Sub-Divisional Officer within 30 days.

The 30-second answer

  1. Apply online on serviceonline.bihar.gov.in (RTPS, General Administration Department), with or without a login, or at the RTPS counter of your block or circle office.
  2. Who issues it: the Circle Officer (Anchaladhikari). A certificate at Sub-Division or District level is issued on the basis of the lower level certificate.
  3. Time limit: 10 working days at each level, and 2 working days for a caste, income or residence certificate under Tatkal service, as listed in the GAD RTPS service list.
  4. Late or rejected? First appeal to the Sub-Divisional Officer within 30 days, then a second appeal to the District Magistrate within 60 days. Appeals are free.
  5. Validity: a caste certificate and a permanent residence certificate stay valid. An income certificate is valid for one year from the date of issue.

How many days does a caste certificate take in Bihar?

The General Administration Department's RTPS service list gives the Circle Officer 10 working days for a caste, income or residence certificate. Under Tatkal service the limit is 2 working days. Public holidays are not counted.

How long does a certificate take in Bihar?

A caste, income or residence certificate in Bihar takes up to 10 working days from the Circle Officer, or 2 working days under Tatkal service. The General Administration Department's RTPS service list sets these limits under the Bihar Right to Public Services Act, 2011, which has applied since 15 August 2011. Under section 5(1) of the Act, the clock starts on the date you submit the application to the designated public servant or to staff authorised to receive it, and you must get an acknowledgement. Rule 6 of the Bihar Right to Public Services Rules, 2011 says public holidays are not counted, so the 10 days are working days. Within the limit the officer must either issue the certificate or reject the application with reasons in writing (section 5(2)). The same 10 working days apply when the Sub-Divisional Officer or the District Magistrate's authorised officer issues the certificate. If the deadline passes with no decision, you can appeal.

How to apply on the RTPS portal

The GAD application process flow gives three ways to apply at Circle level:

  • Online without a login, through the direct service link on serviceonline.bihar.gov.in.
  • Online with a login: fill the form, attach the annexures and submit.
  • Offline at the RTPS counter, where an Executive Assistant enters your form.

Each way gives you an acknowledgement and an SMS and email about the submission. Keep the application reference number. The finished certificate comes as a link in an SMS, an email attachment, in DigiLocker, as a download from Track Application Status on the portal, or from the RTPS counter. To verify a certificate, the portal has a check by submission ID.

How long is each certificate valid?

The General Administration Department's notice on the RTPS portal says:

  • A caste certificate stays valid for good.
  • A permanent residence certificate stays valid for good. A temporary residence certificate is valid for one year from the date of issue.
  • An income certificate is valid for one year from the date of issue.
  • A person's caste is decided by the father's caste, not the husband's.
  • Original documents are returned after verification.
  • For a new non creamy layer certificate, or for a state government job, you can give an undertaking in the set format along with your old non creamy layer certificate instead of applying again each time.

Is my certificate late? Who to appeal to and by when

  1. 1. First appeal

    Official contact
    To the Appellate Authority in the table above: the Sub-Divisional Officer for a Circle level certificate. Use the RTPS Appeal link on serviceonline.bihar.gov.in or apply on paper.
    Deadline
    Within 30 days of the rejection or of the time limit running out (section 6(1)). A late appeal can be admitted for sufficient cause. Decision time in the service list: 15 working days.

  2. 2. Second appeal

    Official contact
    To the Reviewing Authority: the District Magistrate for a Circle level certificate, the Divisional Commissioner for Sub-Division and District level.
    Deadline
    Within 60 days of the first appeal order (section 6(3)). Decision time in the service list: 15 working days.

  3. If the order is not obeyed

    Official contact
    If the officer does not follow the first appeal order to issue the certificate, apply straight to the Reviewing Authority (section 6(4)).
    Deadline
    Not set in the Act.

What the appeal rules say

The Bihar Right to Public Services Rules, 2011 set these points:

  • No fee is charged for a first or second appeal (rule 9).
  • The appeal must give your name and address, the officer's name and address, a summary of the order you appeal against, the grounds and the relief you want (rule 10).
  • Attach a list of documents, a self attested copy of the order and copies of the documents you mention (rule 11).
  • You get notice of the hearing at least 7 days before it (rule 14).

In the appeal the officer has to prove the delay or refusal was justified (section 6(6) of the Act). If the officer failed without good reason, the Appellate Authority can impose a penalty of Rs 250 for each day of delay, up to Rs 5,000, or a lump sum of Rs 500 to Rs 5,000 (rule 8). It is recovered from the officer's salary.

What the state does not publish

The GAD service list and the RTPS pages we checked do not give a fee for these certificates or a full list of supporting documents beyond the forms and affidavits above. Not published by the state on the pages we checked. The RTPS Appeal website did not open for us on 28 October 2026, so use the link on serviceonline.bihar.gov.in or file on paper.

Quick answers

How many days does a caste certificate take in Bihar?

The GAD RTPS service list gives the Circle Officer 10 working days. Under Tatkal service it is 2 working days.

How long is a Bihar income certificate valid?

The General Administration Department's notice on the RTPS portal says an income certificate is valid for one year from the date of issue.

Does a Bihar caste certificate expire?

No. The same notice says a caste certificate stays valid for good, as does a permanent residence certificate. A temporary residence certificate is valid for one year.

Who do I appeal to if my certificate is late in Bihar?

For a Circle level certificate, file a first appeal with the Sub-Divisional Officer within 30 days, then a second appeal with the District Magistrate within 60 days. Appeals are free.

Is there a penalty for the officer?

Yes. Under rule 8 the Appellate Authority can impose Rs 250 for each day of delay, up to Rs 5,000, or a lump sum of Rs 500 to Rs 5,000, recovered from the officer's salary.
