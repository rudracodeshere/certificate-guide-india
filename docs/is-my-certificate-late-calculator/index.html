Is my certificate late? Maharashtra Right to Services date calculator

Last checked: 26 October 2026

For anyone in Maharashtra waiting on an income or domicile certificate.

Enter the date your complete application was received. For a Maharashtra income or domicile certificate, the time limit is 15 days. The calculator shows the due date, the day a first appeal becomes possible, and the last day to file it. Every rule it uses is listed below with its source.

How to use it

  1. Pick the certificate you applied for.
  2. Enter the date on your acknowledgement, when the complete application was received.
  3. If you have already filed a first appeal, enter that date too.
  4. Read your dates. Each line names the rule it comes from.

When can I appeal if my certificate is late?

In Maharashtra you can file a first appeal once the 15 day time limit has passed without the certificate or a written rejection. The appeal must be filed within 30 days of the limit running out. A late appeal can be admitted for sufficient cause, up to 90 days.

Which certificate did you apply for?

Use the date on your acknowledgement. The time limit counts from when the complete application is received.

The rules the calculator uses

Second appealWithin 30 days of the first appeal order, or after 45 days from the first appeal if there is no orderAct section 9(3)
Third appealWithin 60 days of the second appeal orderAct section 18(1)

Calendar days. The Act does not say whether the days are working days, so the calculator counts calendar days. If your last day falls on a holiday, file early. This is a counting aid, not legal advice.

Quick answers

Does the calculator count working days?

No. It counts calendar days, because the Act does not say the days are working days.

Is my data sent anywhere?

No. The dates stay in your browser. The page has no analytics.

Which certificates does it cover?

Maharashtra income and Age, Nationality and Domicile certificates, both with a 15 day time limit.
